Here is the whole talk on one slide; you can come back to it at any point.
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item In 2022 and 2023, De Nederlandsche Bank, DNB, raised the buffer twice, to two percent, while the Basel rulebook pointed to \textbf{zero}.
\item Our verdict: this was \textbf{normalisation, not tightening}. DNB was refilling a buffer, not fighting a boom.
\item It cost banks little, it was largely offset by cuts in other buffers, and we see \textbf{no sign of a contraction in lending}.
\end{itemize}
\par\smallskip
The rest of the talk is the story of how we got there.

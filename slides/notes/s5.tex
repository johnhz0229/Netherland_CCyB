{\small\textbf{Timeline (Zheng, 1:00) [4:30–5:30]}}\par\smallskip
First, the setting, beginning with the timeline.
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item The path was announced in March 2020.
\item In February 2022 DNB published a new framework.
\item In May 2022 it announced one percent; in May 2023 two percent, together with lower buffers for systemically important banks.
\item Both changes bound on the same day: \textbf{31 May 2024}.
\item The buffer has been held at two percent every quarter since, most recently in September 2026.
\end{itemize}
\par\smallskip
Notice the gold band: the ECB's hiking cycle. Both decisions sit right inside it, which is why the timing question is real.

{\small\textbf{The question (Zheng, 1:00) [3:30–4:30]}}\par\smallskip
Fast-forward to 2022. DNB keeps its promise, but look at the timing.
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item War in Ukraine, record energy prices, inflation of \textbf{11.6 percent}, the ECB raising rates from July 2022, and house prices about to turn down.
\item And the Basel indicator says \textbf{zero}.
\end{itemize}
\par\smallskip
\textcolor{navy}{\textbf{[def]}} Raising capital requirements in a downturn is \emph{procyclical}: it amplifies the cycle instead of dampening it. That is precisely the mistake the countercyclical buffer was invented to prevent.
\par\smallskip
So our question is simple: \textbf{was this the wrong moment?} We answer it in three acts, which are the three questions of the assignment: the setting, the decision, and its consequences.
